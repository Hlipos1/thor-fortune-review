    Each round follows the same pattern. A short betting window opens, players lock in their stakes, and the flight begins.     Why players can audit results

    Keeping the balance airborne

    • Set a session budget before the first round and stop when it is used, regardless of the round history.
    • Keep each stake close to one or two percent of the bankroll so a losing stretch does not end the session early.
